How payment works

Paid plans are processed securely through Creem, our merchant of record. You get a standard receipt and can cancel from your Creem account at any time — there is no contract and no per-document fee. The free self-serve engine never requires a purchase or an account.

Credentials stay on your machine. Whether you run Taprun yourself or use the done-for-you service, the automation executes inside your own Chrome on your computer. We never store or transmit your portal passwords — that's the whole point of local-first.

Questions about a plan? Email support@taprun.dev or open an issue at github.com/LeonTing1010/tap/issues.
